## Q34 — Outcome and verdict: the determinate's two registers (Emil's split)

**Proposed.** Discharge always produces a **determinate** (which alternative obtained). It lands in
two registers: as an **outcome** — the determinate as it lands in the world, always produced — and
as a **verdict** — the determinate as assessed by a declared predicate, produced only where
governance has declared one. No declared predicate, no verdict; the world does not render verdicts,
it renders outcomes.

**Consequence, canon-backed:** governance is the conversion of outcomes into verdicts, and every
diachronic instrument runs on verdicts only — the return channel (maturation eats captured
verdicts), the calibration ledger (matured verdicts), carrier assurance (accrues at verdict speed).
This is `DDD-cost-16`'s mechanism named: escaped residual generates no verdict, so it is invisible
to maturation — *escape cannot be learned out of because learning eats verdicts and escape produces
only outcomes.*

**Retro-conversion:** replay over recorded ground retro-judges outcomes into verdicts once a
predicate is declared (retro-minted calibration, predicted-ground note), honest only under
retro-filing's two fields (`DDD-ground-04`). The two-silences distinction survives beneath:
convertible where a verdict function can be declared; floor residual has none in any tense.

**State space:** outcome-only (ungoverned) · verdict-pending (governed, open — the claim layer's
deferred verdict) · verdict-rendered (governed, closed or matured).

**Basis:** `term:verdict`, `DDD-cost-16`, `DDD-frame-12`, `DDD-ground-04`, the individuation
boundary clause. **Falsifier:** a diachronic instrument in canon demonstrated to update on
ungoverned outcomes without a declared predicate anywhere in its loop.
**Routing:** Wave 3 orbit (the term pair files beside Q33-A; "every act's demand meets an outcome;
only governed demand meets a verdict" is A's refined form).

## Q35 — The determinable: what demand applies to (Johnson)

**Proposed, derivation-grade.** Canon defines demand as *what must be supplied* and never names the
object supplied-to. The object: the **determinable** — an outcome-relevant dimension of variation at
the declared tolerance; a **determinate** is a specific way of occupying it; determination is the
passage from one to the other (W.E. Johnson, *Logic* I, ch. XI, 1921). The stack: determinable (the
fuel) → determinate (what discharge produces, in two registers per Q34) → decision (a determinable
taken up by governance) → escape (a determinable resolved by nobody) → **demand = the measure over
unresolved determinables** (`H(V)` where the predicate closes; the verdict variable's support is the
determinate-space at grain τ).

**Structure Johnson supplies, each piece landing on something built:**
1. Determinable–determinate is **not genus–species** — a determinate is a way of being, not the
   determinable plus a differentia. Warrant for the PRD's "axes are properties with ranges" and a
   standing guard against refactoring axes into OWL class hierarchies (Q26).
2. Determinates under one determinable are **constitutively exclusive** — the deep form of
   one-verdict-one-act and store exhaustiveness ("cannot be false").
3. **Orders of determinateness** — red → scarlet → this shade. τ names the order at which the
   framework stops distinguishing; the admission test is the grain-picker; ±50m and ±0.5m are
   different tasks by order, not degree.
4. The determinable is **the dimension of comparability** — what an axis is; the ground registry is
   a registry of determinables.

**Register note:** demand applies to determinables; determinates are what discharge produces. Both
terms enter together, distinct jobs.

**Related work:** Johnson 1921 (source); Prior 1949; Funkhouser 2014; Wilson's SEP survey.
Spencer-Brown as motivation only.
**Falsifier for the term itself:** a canonical use of "demand" that does not parse as a measure over
determinables at a declared grain.
**Routing:** derivation check, then upstream with Wave 3 (the term pair is principle-layer; Paper A's
§2 wants it).

## Q36 — Pull: the schedule of undischarged demand (two forces)

**Proposed.** Demand is a stock; discharge is act-indexed (Q33-B); acts are individuated by what
arrives. **Pull is the gradient of undischarged demand along the arrival schedule**, and it splits
per Q34:

- **World-pull:** outcome arrival on undischarged demand — the fire; the world sets the tempo and
  never misses a tick. Incidents are world-pull at maximum: uncontrolled distribution, short
  consequence horizon, per-tick deterioration.
- **Ledger-pull:** scheduled verdicts — `closesAt`, reviews, expiring certifications — governance
  *manufacturing* verdict arrivals to move the arrangement before world-pull does. Proxy mechanisms
  are manufactured verdict schedules; **Goodhart is ledger-pull mis-aimed** (dragged toward the
  proxy's schedule instead of the task's). Healthy governance is ledger-pull pre-empting world-pull.

**Q9's parked ranking objective, candidate resolution:** expected ungoverned discharge ≈ arrival
rate × residual demand per arrival × consequence gap. Musa profiles rank the first factor; verdict
entropy the second; the pull product is the objective the note said must be named before a ranking
is chosen. High-traffic/zero-demand and quiet/high-demand regions both sort correctly under it.

**Basis:** Q33-A/B, `DDD-cost-25`–`29` (positions, budgets, verdict speed), `DDD-cost-16`, the
streaming result. **Falsifier:** for the ranking claim — regions ranked by the pull product failing
to predict where ungoverned discharge (incident classes, escape findings) concentrates, against
either single-factor baseline.
**Routing:** Q36's definition is prose for Q33's orbit (Paper A motivation, measure-paper discharge
section); the ranking objective stays corpus-gated with Q9 — it is *named* now, not filed.

## Q37 — Ground-indexed determination: the actor as derived object (Emil)

**Proposed — reaches charter vocabulary; handle at dec-09 care level.** The week's four
species-instinct redirections terminate here: determination records are indexed by ground (position,
as-of, delivery, verdict), and **the actor is a projection over the registry** — capability inferred
from the act stream, not posited. Capability = **the region-set where matured-verdict accuracy
clears declared α, membership not magnitude** (the corpus's question-3 finding). Q29's seven
parameters: six are ground-relations; the profile was always a ground-profile wearing a species
name.

**Inference validity conditions, all ratified at v5.5.0:** infer only from acts whose delivery type
is known (else carried encoding is conflated with mechanical arrival — Q15 applied to reading
actors); encoding-proxy as prior, act stream as update, calibration ledger as estimator.

**Two consequences:** `DDD-dec-14` reframes — the identity unit becomes the join key for one
profile, cross-version transfer becomes profile inheritance (partial, per-region) — a smaller
question than the ontological one; annotate dec-14, do not resolve it outside its session. The
ladder experiment becomes the inference run as product: capability-per-region per rung, the knee as
a queryable claim.

**The carve that must ship with it:** the accountable principal refuses the inversion — stake,
sanction, and consequence-bearing are institutional, not inferable from any act stream.
Ground-indexing covers what an arrangement can resolve, never who answers. Register guard verbatim:
profiles describe arrangements; a person is a component; this rates nothing and nobody.

**The repo-name question is flagged and NOT proposed:** whether *actor-indexed determination*
follows the inversion is a charter-level supersession, Emil-only, and the conservative option
(Layer 1 name stands; the inversion files as the diachronic result it is) is strong.
**Falsifier:** an arrangement whose demonstrated capability resists expression as a region-set over
declared axes at any grain — capability not *about* any ground.
**Routing:** downstream (the inference is ledger-machinery through and through), after the Q25/Q27
wave; the dec-14 annotation immediately, one line.

## Q38 — Constitutive priority of ground, and the asymmetry thesis (Emil)

**Two claims, separated by strength:**

**(a) Derivation-grade:** ground description precedes determination *logically* — a decision selects
among determinates, determinates exist only as ways of occupying a determinable, so declaring the
determinable space is prior to any determination over it. F-1's gate already enforces this
mechanically. State the symmetry so the bootstrap is not circular: **ground is prior in each act;
decisions are prior in the registry's growth** (axes enter by naming rulings). Files as a corollary
to Q35.

**(b) Positioning prose (§3.1 discipline — motivation, not claim):** software mechanised the
determinable half from the start (Codd, Chen, type systems, schemas, Evans) and never mechanised the
determination half — schemas everywhere, decision records nowhere; the attachment layer stayed
judgement-carried and escaped. The register completes a seventy-year asymmetry. "Data outlives code"
is weak corroboration (the ground-model half survives its projections). **LLMs' dual role**, stated
precisely: they changed the economics of extraction and of judgement-mediated delivery (enabler) and
made escaped decisions expensive at machine tempo (forcing function, `DDD-agent-01`) — same event,
both directions; rung zero stays model-free (Q32). **Historical anchor: double-entry bookkeeping**
(Pacioli 1494) — declared determinable registry (chart of accounts), as-of-dated acts, a mechanically
closing predicate (books balance); the full stack, five centuries early. Cave paintings stay an
opening image only — under Q30's own diagnostic they are a projection whose source is contested.
**Survey burden if (b) files anywhere:** Codd, Chen, Evans, and the ADR/design-rationale literature —
the one tradition that tried to file decisions, whose failure to mechanise is the delivery gap
v5.5.0 named.

**One-line form:** software mechanised the determinable space and left determination escaped; the
register is the missing half, and it became buildable and urgent in the same year.
**Routing:** (a) with Q35 to Wave 3; (b) to Paper A's introduction / the DDD-method paper.

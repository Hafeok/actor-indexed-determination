## 1. What the paper is

The measure paper is a **formal note**, not a foundation paper. It contributes no mathematics. It
contributes an *identification* — that determination demand is verdict entropy, that conservation is
the chain rule, that the seam is mutual information — and that identification is a modelling claim,
falsifiable, and vindicated on three conditioning variables.

The paper **projects canon and introduces nothing**. Every proposition in it is landed as a claim
node under `core/claims/DDD-measure-*`. Where the prose and a claim disagree, the claim governs and
the prose is the bug — flag it in the claim's `notes:`, never harmonise silently.

**What the paper still owes.** Two genuine standing debts, distinct from staleness.

| Debt | Where booked | State |
|---|---|---|
| Chained seams, multi-actor compositions, non-uniform ground | `core/09` §9 caveat 3; `core/06` line 185 | Open, unmoved. The honest filler for the length gap, if one is ever wanted |
| Outside certification by an information theorist | `core/09` §9 caveat 3; `core/11` §7 | Open, and now owed twice — `core/11` inherited it |

Two further items read as debts and are not. The RAG instance is **not** claimed as evidence of
conservation — `DDD-measure-05` is scoped *"simulation only; a tractability result, not evidence of
conservation"* — and measuring demand on open predicates is a stated boundary, not unpaid work. The
correspondence campaign behind `DDD-measure-07` exists as protocol only: a written falsifier, an
empty evidence list, and a subject matter the boundary charter files downstream, so it cannot be run
from this repository.

## 6. What `core/11` supplies, and where it stops

`core/11-the-floor-mechanism.md` is the paper's nearest dependency, and the place inherited prose most
often gets wrong.

**Landed.**

| Result | Where | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Two capacities | §2 | `C_hold` and `C_resolve`, effective capacity `min(C_hold, C_resolve)` |
| Two overflow modes | §2 | Hold-overflow is encodable away; resolve-overflow is the floor candidate |
| The intersection | §3 | Capacity-generated escape needs overflow **and** open |
| The soft-capacity bound | §4, §4.1 | `p_err = H_b⁻¹(1 − r)`, `r = C_resolve / n`, derived from binary rate–distortion |
| Hallucination as surfaced escape | §6 | Missing, poisoned, or overflowed ground |

**The limit, stated in canon's own words** (`core/11` §7): *"The intersection is sufficient for escape
and is not necessary for it."*

This is `DDD-dec-15`, the scope correction of 2026-08-13, which re-scoped `term:escape-mechanism` and
`DDD-floor-01` by supersession of their universal quantifier. A governing decision that no supplier
took up at all escapes without overflowing anything. Canon diagnoses exactly that on four instances of
ratified canon: `05` §7, `05` §8, `06`'s unanticipated situation at an encoded seam, and `10` §9's
claim layer. Escape as such remains what `term:escape` says — supplied by nobody, for any reason —
and capacity shortfall is *one generator* of it.

**Two consequences for writing.** First, the escape/judgment split is landed for capacity-generated
escape only; write it qualified or not at all. Second, there is exactly one derivation from
rate–distortion in `core/11`, and it is called the **soft-capacity bound**. "Error floor" is not a
name this framework carries, and "floor" is separately owned by `core/03` and by `core/11` §7, where
it means escape that both exceeds capacity and has no verifier.

**Open.** `C_resolve` and `C_hold` are given constants. Deriving them from actor architecture is an
empirical calibration problem, not a proof, and nobody has published the measurement.

**Not open — a boundary, not a debt.** Measuring demand on open predicates. `core/09` measures demand
as verdict entropy, which requires a verdict function; an open predicate is precisely one that lacks
it. This must not appear on any ledger as unpaid work.

**The Kolmogorov argument is the one that must not be misstated.** The claim is that exact
conservation forces entropy over description length, and it rests on symmetry of information holding
only to logarithmic precision — `K(x,y) = K(y) + K(x|y)` up to an additive `O(log n)`, the
Kolmogorov–Levin theorem, published in Zvonkin and Levin 1970 and cited in the literature as `[ZL70]`.
Verified. If that error term is ever restated, re-verify it first.

**Rasch 1960 is registered.** It was added to `meta/lineage-and-limits.md` §6 on 2026-08-14, the gate
pass's one lineage addition. The register in that file is the authoritative citation list for the
programme; consult it before adding any ancestor.

**What is not positioned against, and could be.** Cover and Thomas is the standard reference for the
machinery and is registered in `meta/lineage-and-limits.md` §6, but the paper does not cite it. Sims
2003 (rational inattention) is registered as the source of the channel model of a decision-maker and
belongs to `core/11`'s neighbourhood rather than this paper's.

## 10. Staleness discipline

The failure this projection exists to prevent has a shape, and the shape is general: **a
forward-looking phrase outlives the work it was waiting on.** "Not done", "next result", "pending",
"blocked behind", "the companion framework will" — each is a claim about the future that canon may
already have settled.

Before filing any inherited prose, run the check:

1. List every forward-looking phrase in the draft.
2. For each, name the live section that would have settled it.
3. Read that section and record what it actually says.
4. File only where the draft and the live text agree; hold where they do not.

The audit at `meta/measure-paper-context-staleness-2026-08-14.md` is this check run against the v4.4
projection, and it found the drift in three live places. None was found by reading the draft
carefully. All three were found by reading the referent.
